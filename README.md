# Postwright

**News and blog posts for your WordPress site, on autopilot.**

Postwright is a Windows app that finds the latest stories on the topics you choose, writes them up with AI, publishes them to your WordPress website, and shares them to your social pages. You set it up once and it keeps working in the background.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/laytostudio/postwright-releases/releases/latest)**

---

## What it does

- **News** — follow a topic or a news website. Each new story becomes its own post on your site, rewritten in fresh words.
- **Blog posts** — choose a topic and Postwright writes one original article from several recent stories, with the sources listed underneath.
- **Both at once** — news on some topics, blog posts on others, on the same website.
- **Social sharing** — every published post can go out to your Facebook Page, Instagram and X (Twitter) automatically.
- **Your choice of AI** — works with OpenAI, Claude, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter, or Ollama running free on your own computer. Each one has a step-by-step guide inside the app.
- **You stay in control** — set how many posts go out per day and how far apart, publish automatically or save drafts to review first, and pause everything with one click.
- **Safe Mode** — see what Postwright would publish before anything reaches your website.

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) that stays on while Postwright is working
- A WordPress website where you can log in as an Editor or Administrator
- An account with one AI provider (you pay the provider directly, usually a few cents per post — or use Ollama for free)
- Optional: a Facebook Page, Instagram business account or X account for sharing

## Install

1. Download **Postwright-Setup** (the `.exe` file) from the [latest release](https://github.com/laytostudio/postwright-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open the file. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the app is new — click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Postwright installs and opens on a short setup guide. It walks you through connecting your website, your AI provider and your topics, and tests each connection as you go.

Setup takes about ten minutes.

## Trial and activation

Postwright runs as a **free trial** until you activate it. In the trial it connects to your accounts, finds stories and writes posts, but Safe Mode stays on, so nothing is published, and you see the opening of each post rather than the whole of it. That lets you judge the results on your own topics before you pay.

An **activation key** unlocks publishing for one website and costs **$29 per website, one-time**. There is no subscription.

**To get a key, message [@laytostudio on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/laytostudio/)** with your website address. The app shows you the exact address to send.

## Updates

Postwright updates itself. When a new version is released it downloads in the background and asks you to restart. Your accounts, topics and posts are kept.

## Your data

Everything you enter — passwords, keys, topics, posts — is stored only on your own computer. Postwright sends information only to the services you connect it to: your website, your AI provider and your social accounts.
